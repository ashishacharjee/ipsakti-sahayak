Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 — Section 3(h) Statutory Definition

"Patent or Proprietary medicine" means a formulation containing only ingredients mentioned in the formulae described in the authoritative books of Ayurveda, Siddha or Unani Tibb systems, but which is not a classical formulation specified in First Schedule.

Biological Diversity Act, 2002 — Sections 3 & 7 (NBA/SBB) Biodiversity Mandate

Commercial utilization of biological resources occurring in India requires prior intimation to the concerned State Biodiversity Board (SBB) and payment of Access & Benefit Sharing (ABS) fees ranging from 0.1% to 0.5% ex-factory price.

Indian Patent Act, 1970 — Section 3(p) Non-Patentable TK

Inventions which, in effect, are traditional knowledge or which are an aggregation or duplication of known properties of traditionally known components are non-patentable.
